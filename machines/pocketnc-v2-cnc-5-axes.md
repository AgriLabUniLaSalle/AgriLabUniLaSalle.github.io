# PocketNC v2 - CNC 5 axes

Router CNC 5 axes de petite dimensions

![pocketNC](assets/images/pocketnc.jpg)

![vice](assets/images/vice.png)

Basic informations : http://www.pocketnc.com/v2-getting-started

References Files and tutorials : https://support.pocketnc.com/hc/en-us/categories/360000152287-User-Resources

Modèle de la fixation : http://a360.co/2x36eo4
## Specification

Précision au 0,006mm

B-table offset : 0.835 in

X Axis:

Max Speed: 40 ipm (inches per minute)
Resolution: 0.00024in
Backlash: 0.0005 in at tool base
Deflection at 5 pound load : 0.001 in at tool base
Max Travel: 4.55 in (-2.00 in to 2.55 in) (-50.8 mm to 64.77mm)
Homing Repeatability: +/-0.0005 in
Repeatability: +/-0.002 in at 0% load

Y Axis:

Max Speed: 40 ipm (inches per minute)
Resolution: 0.00024 in
Backlash : 0.0005 in at tool base
Deflection at 8 pound load : 0.001 in at tool base
Max Travel: 5.0 in (-2.5 in to 2.5 in) (-63.5mm to 63.5mm)
Homing Repeatability: +/-0.0005 in
Repeatability: +/-0.002 in at 0% load

Z Axis:

Max Speed: 40 ipm (inches per minute)
Resolution: 0.00024in
Backlash at 18 pound load : 0.0005 in
Deflection at 18 pound load : 0.001 in
Max Travel: 3.55 in (-3.45 in to 0.1 in) (-87.63mm to 2.54mm)
Homing Repeatability: +/-0.0005 in
Repeatability: +/-0.002 in at 0% load

A Axis:

Max Speed: 40°/second
Resolution: 0.004°
Backlash: 0.012°
Deflection at 5 pound load : 0.024° at table edge
Max Travel: -25° to 135°
Homing Repeatability: +/-0.01°
Repeatability: +/-0.01° at 0% load
Runout: less than 0.002"

B Axis:

Max Speed: 40°/second
Resolution: 0.009°
Backlash: 0.01°
Deflection at 5 pound load : 0.024° at table edge
Max Travel: continuous rotation (-9999° to 9999°)
Homing Repeatability: +/-0.01°
Repeatability: +/-0.01° at 0% load
Runout: less than 0.002"

Spindle Speed: 2,000-10,000 rpm 

 

Ethernet, USB, and mini USB
Accepts Standard Gcode
